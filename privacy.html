Privacy Policy — rclone-aegis

rclone-aegis is a personal-use desktop OAuth client used exclusively by its owner to back up local files to the owner's own Google Drive account via rclone.

No third-party users are supported. This application does not collect, store, sell, or share Google user data with any third party. OAuth credentials remain stored locally on the owner's machine, and data transfers occur solely between that machine and Google Drive using the limited drive.file scope.
